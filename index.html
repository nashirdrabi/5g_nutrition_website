General supplement guidance
Is whey protein safe?
Whey is a milk-derived protein with a long history of use in foods and is generally suitable for most healthy adults when used as directed. It is not suitable for someone with a milk-protein allergy, and some lactose-intolerant people may experience symptoms. If you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant, or are unsure, check with your doctor or dietitian first.
Do I need whey in my shake?
No. The shake menu offers a no-whey option and shows a saving of Rs 70 for both sizes. No-whey does not automatically mean dairy-free, so mention any allergy or intolerance while ordering.
Is the food clean?
At 5G, “clean” means an ingredient-led approach built around foods such as fruit, oats, nuts, seeds, yoghurt, eggs, chicken, vegetables, grains, and trout. Individual posters also identify features such as no artificial colours or no preservatives where applicable. Ask for the exact ingredient list if you follow a specific diet.
Is everything made fresh?
The shared menu states that shakes and meals are freshly prepared to order or prepared fresh daily. Availability and preparation times can vary, so confirm your item and preferred time when placing the order.
Are the calories and macros exact?
They are approximate. Ingredient brands, portion sizes, substitutions, and optional additions can change calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. Use the displayed figures as a practical guide rather than a medical calculation.
What if I have a food allergy?
Tell 5G Nutrition before ordering. Menu items may contain or come into contact with milk, whey, yoghurt, nuts, peanuts, seeds, eggs, wheat, mustard, fish, and other allergens. Do not rely on a “no-whey” label as confirmation that an item is dairy-free.
Which option suits my goal?
The menu includes calorie-dense shakes and bowls for higher energy needs, leaner shakes and salads for lighter choices, and high-protein meals for recovery. Your overall diet matters most; for a personalized plan, consult a registered dietitian or qualified nutrition professional.
How do I order and is delivery available?
Send your selection to @5g.nutrition on Instagram. Confirm current availability, customization, total price, payment, and delivery. Delivery is offered around Srinagar within a limited radius.
